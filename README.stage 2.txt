MATHS ARENA — STAGE 2 (CHAPTER NAVIGATION SYSTEM)
=================================================

What is included in Stage 2:
1. Preserved Stage 1 Home Screen:
   - Cream / notebook-inspired visual identity
   - Mathematics Part I (6 chapters) and Mathematics Part II (7 chapters) indexes
   - Analysis and Settings placeholder dialogs
2. Dedicated Chapter Hub View:
   - Displays chapter number, chapter title, and subject (Part I / Part II)
   - Chapter progress bar placeholder (ready for LocalStorage in later stages)
   - Clear entry cards for Tutorial and Question Practice
   - Direct shortcut to take a test for that specific chapter
3. Tutorial Entry Screen & Question Practice Entry Screen:
   - Dedicated sub-views for every chapter
   - Preview difficulty selector on the Question Practice entry screen
   - Easy switching between Tutorial, Question Practice, and Chapter Hub
4. Test Arena Section & Selection Interface:
   - Accessible directly from the Home Screen (no tutorial completion required)
   - Supports Individual Chapter (all 13 chapters), Mathematics Part I, Mathematics Part II, and Full Syllabus
   - Difficulty level selector and live configuration summary
5. Universal & Browser Back Navigation:
   - Sticky topbar Back button across all sub-pages
   - Full browser/Android Back button support via History API (pushState / popstate)
